Cambo Woodlands Circular Walk


Description of the walk

This walk takes you from the Stables Visitor Centre heading south past the car park and along a flat, narrow in places, woodland path towards the entrance to the estate. Crossing the main driveway, we head down a sloping path towards the Cambo Burn. Keeping the burn on our right we follow the path as it meanders through the Cambo Den. Take note of the interesting rock formations and tree root systems on the other side of the Den.

Continue along before crossing over a bridge* and taking the left fork thereby now having the burn on our left. As you walk along this section keep a lookout for the badger sett on the opposite bank of the burn.

*Note: some of the bridges only have handrails on the one side!

Just before the next bridge, we take a sharp right, up a fairly steep incline, through a gate, that brings us to the edge of the woodland.

We follow the path along the side of the field, past the compost bins, and re- enter the woodland where we continue down the path, keeping the burn on our left. Along this path we pass a fence with a gate, which is an ideal place for Strength & Balance exercises. Beyond this fence we come to a bridge where we cross over the burn and turn left in the direction of the stables.

Follow this path, keeping the burn to your left. Look out for the otter holt on the south bank of the burn.

Through the gate, we tum right and the walk ends after we skirt round the north of the Walled Garden, through the Prairie Garden and back to the Stables Visitor Centre.

Pathway Conditions

The gravel path is clear and wide enough for a wheeled walking aid. There are some inclines, both down and up, mainly on the return section, where extra care is necessary, particularly on the downward inclines

Weather Considerations

In Autumn, some of the paths have a good covering of leaves that can be slippery when wet.

In winter, the path may be slippery in places during frosty weather however remains mostly passable.

Safety Considerations

The walk is generally safe, however, care should be taken on the boardwalks and in wet weather.

The walk crosses a road at the entrance to the estate and walkers should take care when crossing.

The walk passes a golf course and walkers should be aware of golfers playing and move along quietly.

About the Walks

These walks have been developed by the Monday Walking Group who meet at the Cambo Stables each Monday morning at 9.30am, weather permitting.

Group walks are in association with Walking Scotland, a National Walking and Wheeling charity, whose purpose is to encourage walking by providing a framework for organised walking groups.

Walking Scotland use both terms walking and wheeling as it’s more inclusive. Both words represent the action of moving at a pedestrian’s pace, whether or not someone is unaided or using some kind of wheeled mobility aid.

The Walk Leaders have received appropriate training to ensure the walks are suitable and risk assessed for all attending, including those who use wheeled devices.

The walks are short, safe and social where the aim is to walk at a pace that we are able to talk. They last approximately 1 → 1¼ hours and between 1 → 5km in length.

Depending on weather conditions, the paths may be slippery in places.
