Cambo Estate Walk - 5.4Km


Description of the walk route

This walk takes you from the Stables Visitor Centre heading south past the car park and along a flat woodland path towards the entrance to the estate where it takes a right tum and heads through woodland on the inside of the estate perimeter wall. There are a. number of small boardwalks on this section where care should be taken, particularly if wet.

Walking through this part of the woodland there are fields to the right where roe deer can be seen crossing and grey herons are regular visitors, the nests of which are in the treetops above you.

Further along this path is an old Icehouse, previously used to store fresh meat, fish, fruit and ice throughout the year before mechanical refrigeration was invented. Beyond the icehouse the path undulates and narrows, due to the vegetation, where single file walking is required. Further along, on the left-hand side, is a rope swing that was used by the legendary Annmarie Taggart on her Cambo Health Walks.

At the end of this path, exit through a wooden gate, go cross the road, and enter another woodland again through a wooden gate. Follow the ingle-file path along the left side of a mill pond and continue until a tarmac road is reached. This is the vehicle entrance road to Kingsbams GC. Take a right and walk along the right hand side grass verge until the golf practice area is in view. Immediately before the practice area, take the path on the left, along the edge of the field with the woodland on your right. Keep going until you reach a tarmac road ( Back Stile), where you tum right and it leads down to a car park.

Situated at one end of this car park is Harbour Burger (fast food & refreshments) and an area that can be used for Strength & Balance exercises.

Head to the far end of the car park, go through a kissing gate and follow the track between the beach and golf course; when the track curves away to the right, fork left on a grassy path (waymarker). Be aware of any golf being played and move along quietly.

Before reaching a band of trees the route emerges onto a wider track; bear right along this, passing to the right of the wooden toilets, Beyond these the track enters the woods. Go through the Cambo gate ahead signed for Fife Coastal Path, note the bridge over the Cambo burn but do not cross this, instead turn right up path on the near bank.

Follow this path, keeping the bum to your left. Look out for the otter holt on the opposite bank of the burn.

Further along, with Cambo House on your right, the path reaches a wooden gate.

Through the gate, we tum right and the walk ends after we skirt round the north of the Walled Garden, through the Prairie Garden and back to the Stables Visitor Centre.

Pathway Conditions

The gravel path at the start of the walk is clear and wide enough for a wheeled walking aid. Through the woodland, adjacent to the estate perimeter wall, the path has undulations and can be soft underfoot. From the woodland down to the Kingsbams Beach Car Park, the path remains firm in most weathers.

Beyond the car park, the path is compacted sand and, therefore, prone to becoming soft in wet weather.

Again, on the woodland walk back to the Stables the path can be soft underfoot.

Weather Considerations

In Autumn, some of the paths have a good covering of leaves that can be slippery when wet.

In winter, the path may be slippery in places during frosty weather however remains mostly passable.

About the Walks

These walks have been developed by the Monday Walking Group which meets at the Cambo Estate each Monday morning at 9:30, weather permitting. The walks take approximately one hour and are between 2 and 3 kilometres in length.

The group walks in association with Walking Scotland, a charity whose purpose is to encourage walking by providing a framework for organised walking groups. The walk leaders have received appropriate training to ensure the walks are suitable and safe for all. Many of the walks are also suitable for people who walk with wheeled devices.

The Cambo Estate is managed by the Cambo Heritage Trust, and operated to maintain the historic house, grounds and associated buildings and gardens in environmentally sustainable format for all to enjoy.
